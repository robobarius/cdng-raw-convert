# Code of Conduct

cDNG Raw Convert is a small, friendly project made by camera people for camera people. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to GitHub, programming or the SIGMA fp.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Explain why, and suggest a better way.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change, and why.
- Respect people's footage and privacy: don't share someone else's clips or personal details without their permission.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you've been asked to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may block people who keep breaking it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their GitHub profile ([@robobarius](https://github.com/robobarius)), or open an issue if it doesn't need to be private. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in the project's spaces on GitHub (issues, pull requests, discussions) and wherever someone represents the project.
